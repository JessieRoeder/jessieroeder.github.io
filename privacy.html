Privacy policy

This policy covers the Google OAuth client named "rclone" registered by Jessie Roeder.

  • The client is for the owner's personal use only, with the open-source rclone tool running on the owner's own computer.
  • It requests only the drive.file permission: access to files that rclone itself creates or opens in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see other files.
  • Files and sign-in tokens stay between the owner's computer and Google. No data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with anyone else, and this website stores nothing.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
